What the service handles

The public demo processes your verified Google identity and email for authentication, demo enrollment and your optional communication preferences. It stores the sources you upload, their original files, extracted passages, wiki pages, revisions, citations, publications and audit history in your private personal space. Wiki content is not encrypted end to end: the service and its operators can technically access it, so use only sample material.

Retention

Demo accounts and wiki data
Reset daily at 00:00 UTC, including original files, databases and their disposable replicas. The daily reset does not remove exports or files already on your device.
Email without marketing consent
Retained separately for up to 30 days after your last demo use.
Consented marketing contact details and preferences
Retained separately from the demo wiki, reviewed after 12 months, and removable on withdrawal subject to any necessary minimal suppression or consent-evidence obligations.
Operational logs
Retained for up to 30 days, with a limited documented investigation period for security incidents. Logs must exclude authentication credentials and wiki content.

Isolated recovery backups

Nonmarketing contact records and ordinary security logs are removed from the live service after their stated 30-day retention periods. Copies in isolated recovery snapshots may remain for up to an additional 30 days before expiry. These backups are restricted to recovery and are not available for ordinary marketing use. Restoration must not reactivate old marketing permissions. This allowance applies to durable contact and security records only; demo wiki data and its replicas remain subject to the daily reset.

Your choices

Commercial contact and newsletter consent are optional and separate. Declining both does not prevent demo access. You can withdraw both choices after enrollment, or with a private withdrawal link without signing in. Minimal purpose-specific suppression records are retained separately to honor your withdrawal; signing in again or resetting the demo does not reverse it.
