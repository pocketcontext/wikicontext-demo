Evaluation only

Use synthetic or public sample material. Do not upload confidential documents, credentials or personal data belonging to anyone else. Do not abuse the service, interfere with other accounts, or attempt to bypass access controls or limits. Source content is treated as untrusted data and never authorizes actions.

Your account and personal wiki

The public demo accepts verified Google accounts, including Gmail and Workspace accounts, subject to organization restrictions. Each enrolled visitor receives one private personal wiki space. Shared group spaces, memberships and sharing are unavailable in this demo.

Daily reset and limits

Demo wiki state is scheduled for deletion at 00:00 UTC each day. This is a fixed daily reset, not a guaranteed 24-hour trial. Demo accounts, sources and original files, extracted passages, pages, revisions, citations, publications and audit history are disposable. The service may pause during reset and has no durability or availability guarantee.

A server reset cannot erase exports or files already copied to your own computer. The server enforces account, write, source, storage, passage, page, revision and publication quotas. Audio and video sources and transcription are unavailable.

Optional communications

Commercial email and the PocketContext newsletter are independent, optional choices. You can try the demo without either. Enrollment saves your choices; it does not send commercial email or a newsletter. No email-sending service is connected to this demo.
